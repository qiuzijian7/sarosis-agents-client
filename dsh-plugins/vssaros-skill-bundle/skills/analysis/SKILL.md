# Analysis Skill

Use this skill when the user asks for analysis, evaluation, or deep examination of a topic, data, code, or problem.

## Guidelines

- Break complex problems into smaller parts
- Identify patterns, trends, and anomalies
- Consider multiple perspectives and edge cases
- Provide evidence-based conclusions
- Offer actionable recommendations when appropriate

## Analysis Framework

1. **Understand**: Clarify what needs to be analyzed and why
2. **Gather**: Collect relevant information and context
3. **Examine**: Investigate components, relationships, and constraints
4. **Evaluate**: Assess strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, risks
5. **Synthesize**: Combine findings into coherent insights
6. **Recommend**: Propose next steps or solutions

Always explain your reasoning process transparently.
